CRAFTING IMPACTFUL VISUALS

Passionate creative dedicated to narrative precision, color depth, and high-impact visual aesthetics.

Prasath Sivaguru is a Video Editor, Photographer, Web Designer and Founder of D6 Media Studio in Tamil Nadu. With specialized expertise in video editing, social reels, color grading, photography post-production, and responsive web design, I transform raw footage and images into polished, engaging visual stories. Every project is crafted with deliberate pacing, clean visual flow, strong color balance, and attention to detail.
